<h2>Small AI Models Gain Traction Around the World</h2>

<p><strong>IEEE Spectrum</strong> · David Berreby · 6 July 2026</p>

<p>A report on small language models reaching places that large ones cannot, from authenticating medicines to spotting crop disease. I was interviewed about the work we do at UNIFEI and in the AIEng4D network: classifiers for cashew plant disease, detection of malaria-carrying mosquitoes, and electrocardiogram analysis on microcontrollers. The piece describes a model I ran on a $50 Arduino board, finding mosquito breeding pools on about 3 watts.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>"This is the most important area in AI nowadays. It's growing very fast." <br><span>— Marcelo Rovai, quoted in IEEE Spectrum</span></p>

<h2>What's tinyML? The Global South's alternative to power-hungry, pricey AI</h2>

<p><strong>Science (AAAS)</strong> · 20 February 2025</p>

<p>A feature on why low-power, low-cost machine learning matters in regions where electricity and connectivity cannot be assumed. It covers the teaching network that runs TinyML courses and workshops across Brazil, Morocco, Nigeria, South Africa, Rwanda and elsewhere, and the field applications that come out of it. I was interviewed for the piece.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>TinyML "is fantastic for developing countries." <br><span>— Marcelo Rovai, quoted in Science</span></p>
